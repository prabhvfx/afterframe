# The Seventh Room: Master Production Bible

Original fiction. 4 vertical parts (40 to 55 s) and one YouTube long-form cut.
Genre: slow-burn domestic horror told through floor plans, measurements and
night-time sound. No gore. The fear comes from numbers that don't add up.

## Logline

An architect buys a cheap old house whose listing promises seven rooms. She
counts six. The missing room is inside the walls, and something in it knows
the house better than she does.

## Rules of the haunting (keep consistent)

1. The knocking always comes at 3:12 AM. Always three knocks.
2. The gap between the walls is 90 cm when found. It grows 1 cm every night.
3. The Occupant copies. Whatever is done to the wall, it does back.
4. It keeps a floor plan of the house, updated nightly, and draws the people in it.

## Part 1 (built): The Gap

The listing says seven rooms. Nadia counts six. She measures the hallway:
4.20 m inside, 5.10 m outside, 90 cm with no door. Theo jokes it off. At 3:12,
three knocks from inside the wall. In the morning she drills a hole. Cold air
comes out. Then something knocks back, right where the drill was.

Cliffhanger: what is in the gap.

## Part 2 (built, 49.5 s): The Camera

Recap: 90 cm, three knocks, a hole. Nadia feeds a phone inspection camera
through the hole. A narrow room. A chair facing the wall. A desk. On the desk,
a floor plan of their house, in pencil. Two figures drawn in the bedrooms. Theo
laughs it off: "Previous owner was an architect too, I guess." Nadia zooms in.
The pencil drawing has Theo in a blue hoodie. He bought it yesterday.

Cliffhanger: the plan was drawn after they moved in.

## Part 3 (built, 54.4 s): Mrs. Albrecht

Recap: a plan with them in it. Nadia calls the previous owner's daughter.
Mrs. Albrecht: "Father bricked up that room in 1974. He said it was for the
tenant." There was never a tenant on any lease. Nadia measures again: 91 cm.
92. 93. Every night, one more centimeter. Theo starts sleeping through the
3:12 knocks. One night his bed is empty and the hallway carpet is pulled
toward the wall.

Cliffhanger: Theo is gone at 3:12.

## Part 4: Seven

Recap: a growing gap, an empty bed. Nadia finds Theo asleep in the hallway,
cheek against the wall, whispering to it. She opens the wall with a sledge.
The room is empty, the chair still warm. On the desk, the floor plan, updated:
seven rooms now. Six drawn figures in a house with two people. The last line of
pencil is still being drawn, toward Nadia's room.

End: "Full story on YouTube."

## Visual language

- Night scenes: navy and black, a single flashlight cone, grain up.
- Day scenes: blueprint grid, paper listings, dimension lines in yellow.
- Red only means the Occupant: knocks, the gap, the drawings.
- Characters are flat vector busts. They blink. Mouths move only on their own lines.

## Sound language

- Horror bed: low beating drone (41 Hz vs 43.6 Hz) with a thin high tone.
- Knocks are dry wood with room tail. Three, evenly spaced, never four.
- Silence before every knock.

# Afterframe brand kit

Rendered from code: `npx remotion still src/index.ts BrandAvatar brand/avatar.png`
and `BrandBanner brand/banner.png` (source: `src/brand/BrandStills.tsx`).

- `avatar.png` 1080x1080. The mark: a paper frame with an "A", the next frame
  (red) behind it. Checked at 110 px in a circle crop.
- `banner.png` 2560x1440 for YouTube. Logo and tagline sit inside the
  1546x423 center area every device shows.
